## 1. Information We Collect  

### 1.1 Automatically Collected Data  
- **Device & Browser Data**: IP address (anonymized), browser type, OS version, and device identifiers for analytics (e.g., crash reporting).  
- **Usage Data**: Pages visited, session duration, and interactions (processed locally, not stored on servers).  

### 1.2 User-Provided Data  
- **Preferences**: Language and theme settings (stored locally via cookies/LocalStorage).  
- **Communications**: If you contact us (e.g., via email), we collect your email address and message content.  

### 1.3 No Cloud Storage  
CubeVisage operates **fully client-side**—your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly share it.

## 2. How We Use Your Data  
We process data to:  
- Provide core functionality (e.g., theme/language persistence).  
- Analyze usage trends (anonymous, aggregated statistics).  
- Respond to support requests.  
- Comply with legal obligations (e.g., EU GDPR, U.S. CCPA).  

**Legal Basis (GDPR)**:  
- **Consent** (for cookies/local storage).  
- **Legitimate Interest** (security, analytics).

## 3. Cookies & Local Storage  
We use:  
- **Essential Cookies**: Store language and theme preferences.  
- **LocalStorage**: Retain temporary settings (e.g., form drafts).  

**Control**: Disable cookies via browser settings, but some features may break. We **do not** use tracking cookies or third-party analytics[citation:4][citation:7].

## 4. Data Storage & Security  
- **Local-Only**: All data resides on your device (no server uploads).  
- **Security Measures**: Encryption (where applicable), but users must protect their own devices.  
- **Retention**: Data persists until you clear browser storage or reset settings.

## 5. Data Sharing & Disclosure  
We **do not** sell or share data with third parties, except:  
- **Legal Requests**: If required by law (e.g., subpoenas).

## 6. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)  
Under GDPR/CCPA, you may:  
- **Access/Delete**: Clear data via browser settings.  
- **Correct**: Update preferences directly on the Website.  
- **Portability**: Export settings manually (no automated tool required).  
- **Withdraw Consent**: Disable cookies/local storage.  

Contact `wisebreeze@163.com` for requests[citation:5][citation:10].
